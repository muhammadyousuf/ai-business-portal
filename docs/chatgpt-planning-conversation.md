## The problem you're solving

Businesses lose customers because:

- They cannot respond to every message immediately.
- Customers ask the same questions repeatedly.
- Staff may miss messages outside business hours.
- Customers have to wait for prices, availability or delivery information.
- Orders and inquiries are scattered across multiple platforms.
- Owners have limited visibility into customer conversations.

Your platform will bring these interactions into one place and automate the repetitive work.

# 7. AI engine kaise kaam karega?

Is product ka sabse important part AI nahi, balkay AI ko correct business information aur allowed actions dena hai.

Main hybrid approach recommend karta hoon:

**A. Knowledge retrieval (RAG)**

Customer poochta hai: "Aapke school mein admission ki last date kya hai?"

System:
1. Customer ka question receive karega.
2. Business ki knowledge base se relevant information search karega.
3. AI ko relevant information provide karega.
4. AI us information ke mutabiq jawab dega.
5. Reliable answer na milne par human handover karega.

**B. Structured data and tools**

Customer poochta hai: "2 kg chocolate cake kitne ka hai?"

AI products collection se price fetch karega. Price khud generate nahi karega.

AI ko controlled functions provide karein, jaise:

- `searchProducts`
- `getProductPrice`
- `checkDeliveryAvailability`
- `calculateDeliveryCharges`
- `createOrderRequest`
- `requestAppointment`
- `handoverToHuman`

AI in functions ko sirf authorized business context mein execute kar sake.

**C. Workflow engine**

Har business apne workflows configure kar sake. Example:

`Customer inquiry → AI response → Order request → Owner notification → Human confirmation`

Ye architecture aapko future mein different industries add karne dega, bina har dafa AI system dobara banaye.

# 9. Monthly subscription pricing

Aap monthly subscription model chahte hain. Main international customers ke liye USD pricing aur Pakistan ke liye PKR equivalent pricing suggest karunga.

Ye starting hypotheses hain jinhein customer interviews aur paid pilots ke baad adjust karna hoga.

| Plan | Monthly price | Suggested limits |
|---|---:|---|
| Starter | $19 | 1 website, 300 AI replies |
| Growth | $49 | 2 channels, 1,000 AI replies |
| Pro | $99 | 4 channels, 3,000 AI replies |
| Business | $199 | Higher limits, multiple staff |

Features aur limits ko actual API costs ke hisaab se adjust karein. "AI replies" ko clearly define karein: ek customer message ka ek AI-generated response, not every token or internal retrieval operation.

Pakistan ke liye aap equivalent PKR plans bhi rakh sakte hain, magar international pricing ko blindly exchange-rate convert na karein. Local market ke payment preferences aur affordability alag ho sakti hai.

**Very important:** Subscription mein unlimited AI usage, unlimited messages ya unlimited WhatsApp notifications na dein. API aur messaging costs aapke margin ko khatam kar sakti hain.

# 13. Important business risks

| Risk | Solution |
|---|---|
| AI wrong price bataye | Database-backed price lookup |
| AI unknown answer invent kare | Confidence threshold + human handover |
| WhatsApp integration fail ho | Webhook logs and retry mechanism |
| Business data leak ho | Tenant isolation and access controls |
| AI API cost high ho | Usage limits, caching and cheaper models |
| Customer messages miss hon | Queue, retries and delivery logs |
| Business staff response na de | Pending inquiry alerts |
| Industry requirements different hon | Configurable workflows and templates |

Security ko MVP se hi seriously lena hoga. Especially schools aur clinics mein personal data aa sakta hai. Sensitive information ko unnecessarily collect na karein, permissions enforce karein, aur relevant privacy laws ke mutabiq data retention aur deletion controls implement karein.

# 14. Product ko competitors se different kaise banayein?

Sirf chatbot banana enough differentiation nahi hai. Website chat widgets aur generic AI assistants pehle se available hain.

Aapka unique selling proposition ye hona chahiye:

**"An AI employee that understands your business, handles customer inquiries, collects orders and bookings, and connects every conversation to your business workflow."**

Meri recommendation hai ke aap ye differentiators build karein:

1. **Industry templates:** Restaurant, bakery, school, clinic, ecommerce etc.
2. **Action-based AI:** Sirf jawab nahi, order aur booking request create kare.
3. **Multi-channel inbox:** Different channels ki conversations ek dashboard mein.
4. **Business-controlled knowledge:** Owner khud prices, policies aur information update kare.
5. **Human handover:** AI aur real staff ek hi conversation continue kar sakein.
6. **Simple onboarding:** Business owner bina developer ke assistant configure kar sake.
